## Ehrlichiosis and Anaplasmosis in Dogs and Cats

### Definition

- **Ehrlichiosis and Anaplasmosis** are tick-borne rickettsial diseases caused by **Ehrlichia** and **Anaplasma**.
    
- **Dogs:** ==Obligate intracellular== pathogens in three genera: **==Ehrlichia==, ==Anaplasma==, ==Neorickettsia==**.
    
    - **Ehrlichia:**
        
        - _E. canis_: Canine ==Monocytic== Ehrlichiosis (**CME**)
            
        - _E. ewingii_: ==Granulocytic== Ehrlichiosis
            
        - _E. chaffeensis_: Primarily human pathogen, may rarely affect dogs
            
    - **Anaplasma:**
        
        - _A. ==phagocytophilum_==: Granulocytic Anaplasmosis
            
        - _A. ==platys_==: ==Thrombocytic== Anaplasmosis
            
- **Cats:** Feline ehrlichiosis is ==rare==; serologic, PCR, and cytologic evidence exist for **Ehrlichia** (_E. canis, E. ewingii, E. chaffeensis_) and **Anaplasma** (_A. phagocytophilum_). Clinical signs: fever, lethargy, joint pain, anemia, hyperglobulinemia, thrombocytopenia, or asymptomatic.
